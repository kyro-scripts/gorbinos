 TO PLAY:      Run GORBINOSQUEST.EXE. No installation required.
               Saves are kept in %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Gorbino's Quest

 REQUIREMENTS: 64-bit Windows, a video card with OpenGL 3.3, a mouse.

 CONTROLS
   W A S D ........ MOVE              MOUSE ........... LOOK
   LEFT CLICK ..... FIRE              E / RIGHT CLICK . USE (DOORS, SWITCHES, LIFTS)
   SPACE .......... JUMP              SHIFT ........... SPRINT
   R .............. RELOAD / VENT     1-7 / WHEEL ..... WEAPONS
   F / MIDDLE ..... KNIFE: SLASH, GUT UP CLOSE, OR WHIP ONE IN FROM AFAR
   C .............. SLIDE
   Q .............. LAST WEAPON       TAB ............. AUTOMAP
   F5 / F9 ........ QUICK SAVE/LOAD   ESC ............. MENU
   All keys can be rebound in OPTIONS > CONTROLS.

 NOTES
   * Walls with a slightly different texture can often be opened. Press USE.
   * Health can go above 100.
   * The plasma rifle overheats. Press RELOAD to vent it early.
   * Rockets damage you too, and push you.
   * Checkpoints save your progress when you touch them.
   * Nothing can hurt you while you are gutting something.
   * The crosshair flashes white on a hit and red on a kill.
   * The little pastel Gorbinos in smocks are locals. They don't fight.
   * Kill fast to fill the FRENZY meter (the fish skin under your health).
   * That eye in the top-left corner is yours. It looks toward whatever hit you.
   * ENDLESS on the title screen: waves in the Gorb Pit until you drop.

 DIFFICULTY
   MERCY .......... less damage taken, double ammo
   STANDARD ....... the intended difficulty
   CRUELTY ........ faster enemies, heavier damage
   HELL ........... no room for error
