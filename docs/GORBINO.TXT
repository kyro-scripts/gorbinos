 THANK YOU FOR PURCHASING GORBINO'S QUEST!

 TO PLAY:      Double-click GORBINOSQUEST.EXE. No installation required.
               Your saves live in %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Gorbino's Quest

 SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
   - Any 64-bit Windows PC with an OpenGL 3.3 capable video card
   - A mouse (two buttons recommended, three buttons decadent)
   - Sound card (recommended; the Gorbinos have a lot to say)

 CONTROLS
   W A S D ........ MOVE              MOUSE ........... LOOK
   LEFT CLICK ..... FIRE              E / RIGHT CLICK . USE (DOORS, SWITCHES)
   SPACE .......... JUMP              SHIFT ........... SPRINT
   R .............. RELOAD / VENT     1-6 / WHEEL ..... WEAPONS
   Q .............. LAST WEAPON       TAB ............. AUTOMAP
   F5 / F9 ........ QUICK SAVE/LOAD   ESC ............. MENU
   (everything can be rebound in OPTIONS > CONTROLS)

 HINTS FROM THE GLANDCORE HOTLINE
   * Walls that look a little different usually ARE different. Press USE.
   * Gorb Juice can push your health past 100. So can the Gorb Orb.
   * Red Gorbinos do not shoot. They do not need to.
   * The SNIRB-O-TRON overheats. Press RELOAD to vent it early.
   * Rockets hurt you too. Rockets also make you fly. Do with this what you will.
   * Barrels of GORB-COLA are not for drinking.
   * The spinning green heads are checkpoints. They save your game.
   * Feeling lost? Press TAB.

 THE GORB OF ALL KNOWLEDGE (DIFFICULTY)
   BABY GORB ...... half damage, double ammo
   GORB ENJOYER ... the way Gorbino intended
   ULTRA-GORB ..... faster, angrier Gorbinos
   GORB HELL ...... not even remotely fair
